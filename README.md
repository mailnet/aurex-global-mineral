# Aurex Global Mineral

Build a luxury corporate website preview and demo for AUREX GLOBAL MINERALS (tagline: "Precious Resources. Professional Relationships."), an international gold and diamond trading company operating between Kinshasa and Dubai with global escort logistics.

Incorporate the attached brand logo designs (the gold faceted 'A' monogram) and the luxury palette: Obsidian Black (#0A0A0A), Champagne Gold (#C6A15B), and Ivory (#F5F2EB).

The website should feature a full 6-page architecture with a rich, immersive homepage containing 15 to 20 structured sections:
1. Top Bar & Global Header: Multi-currency status ticker, language toggle (English / French), and direct 'Institutional Trade Desk' CTA.
2. Hero Section: Cinematic dark aesthetic, the faceted gold monogram logo, headline ("Direct Sourcing & Secure International Custody of Pure Gold and Fine Diamonds"), and dual action buttons.
3. Operating Corridor Ticker: Highlighting the Kinshasa Sourcing Basin and Dubai Commodity Hub (DMCC/Airport Freezone).
4. Commodities Showcase: High-grade Gold (Doré bars & 999.9 Bullion) and Diamonds (Rough & Polished).
5. The Kinshasa–Dubai Pipeline (Interactive Process Map): Mine-to-market flow (Sourcing -> Assay Verification -> Bonded Transit -> Global Delivery).
6. The Escort & Custody Protocol: Explaining the global physical escort, transit insurance, armed logistics, and advance operational clearance in enterprise-grade language.
7. Due Diligence & Compliance Framework: OECD Due Diligence alignment, Kimberley Process Certification Scheme (KPCS), and strict KYC/AML protocols.
8. Trust & Transaction Assurance Model: Step-by-step transaction security addressing client trust (independent assaying, regulated escrow, secure vault handover).
9. Product Specification Matrix: Detailed breakdown of purity grades, assay standards, minimum order quantities (MOQ), and packaging.
10. Sourcing Ethics & Origin Tracking: Traceability and responsible mineral sourcing in the DRC.
11. Secure Facilities & Custody Vaults: Overview of secure storage and custody partners.
12. Institutional Buyer Profiles: Tailored pathways for private wealth, jewelers, refiners, and international trading desks.
13. Procedural Walkthrough: From Letter of Intent (LOI) and Full Corporate Offer (FCO) to laboratory inspection and final settlement.
14. Corporate Philosophy & Leadership: Discretion, long-term relationships, and contractual reliability.
15. Frequently Asked Questions: Transparent answers on inspections, advance transit logistics fees, and export documentation.
16. Trade Desk RFP & Inquiry Portal: Encrypted inquiry form with commodity selection, volume estimate, and direct WhatsApp Business routing.
17. Global Office Locations & Contact Grid: Kinshasa Office and Dubai Trade Desk details.
18. Institutional Footer: Legal notices, compliance disclosures, and full navigation.

Provide navigable routes for all 6 pages:
- / (Home)
- /commodities (Gold & Diamond specifications)
- /logistics (Escort, Security & Transit)
- /compliance (Kimberley Process, OECD & KYC)
- /about (The House & Heritage)
- /inquiry (Trade Desk & WhatsApp Form)
